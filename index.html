FROM THE FOUNDING RECORD

Purpose before prestige.
People before power.

The Adara Principle began as a question about the price of personal ambition, then grew into a philosophy of responsibility without inherent rulers, justice tempered by mercy, and meaningful work in service of the people who reside on this Earth.

Adara concerns how we treat one another. It neither prescribes belief in God nor asks anyone to abandon their faith. Believers may understand these duties as care for the Creator’s creation; people of other beliefs or none may follow them on their own terms. Earth is our shared home, not an object of worship.
