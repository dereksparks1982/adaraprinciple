# Founding Record — October 16, 2026

This is a thematic summary of the founding discussion, **not a verbatim transcript**. It records what was adopted and which implementation questions were explored.

**The Everest challenge.** The conversation opened with the morality of status-driven climbing when other people face risk. It established that making money or jobs does not, by itself, demonstrate social benefit.

**The opium-trade analogy.** Afghanistan's opium economy was invoked to show that profitable economic activity can coexist with devastating harm. The argument was about the limits of a jobs-only defense, not an assertion that mountain tourism is equivalent to drug trafficking.

**A philosophy instead of a fictional state.** The working name Adara emerged, then the project shifted away from a fictional country and toward voluntary teachings that do not establish an object of worship.

**Tenets I–V.** Common Benefit, Contribution, Betterment, Reciprocity, and Persuasion were developed and adopted. The founding belief was that no person has an inherent right to rule over another.

**Tenet VI, Sovereignty.** A story about someone imagining himself a local strongman in a collapse scenario prompted a principle rejecting violent domination.

**Tenet VII, Communal Justice.** Community-wide judgment, severity-based consequences, repeat offenses, second chances, and restoration were discussed. The seventh tenet was expressly accepted. Elders and checks against bias became central topics.

**The repository.** The public repository [adaraprinciple](https://github.com/dereksparks1982/adaraprinciple) was established to preserve the tenets, their explanations, sources, and a GitHub Pages website.

**An intellectual lineage.** The site was asked to show how altruism, communitarianism, philosophical anarchism, generational duties, restorative justice, and other traditions mix within Adara, including important disagreements.

**Tenet VIII, Shared Provision.** The later conversation explored common provision of grain and other essentials, meaningful work without ordinary wages, and each person's chance to pursue a vocation suited to their passions and abilities. The founder emphasized removing arbitrary obstacles and prioritizing real human needs on Earth over costly prestige projects such as space exploration. These thoughts inform the added tenet and its detailed [economic commentary](ECONOMY.md).

**Development convention.** The founder directed that new tenets be taken as accepted unless explicitly rejected or held for reconsideration. This living philosophy may be revised, but the text of the first six tenets is preserved.

**Clarification of purpose — October 22, 2026.** The founder clarified that Adara is about conduct toward people who reside on Earth, not worship of the Earth or rejection of God. Believers may understand Adara’s duties as care for the Creator’s creation, while participation remains open to people of any or no faith. The public-facing description was revised from “in service of the Earth” to “in service of the people who reside on this Earth.” The eight adopted tenets remain unchanged.
